## Editorial conventions

- Keep the service glossary consistent across pages, forms and footer; use the navigation gettext context for short header labels.
- Use accompagner, coordonner, mettre en relation and assurer le suivi where the Chinese describes assistance. English uses support, coordinate, assist and facilitate.
- Keep approved Chinese line spans. Translate linked hero lines together for natural French and English phrasing.
- Language names in the switcher are autonyms: 中文 / Français / English. Desktop uses 中文 / FR / EN.
- The internal consultation email stays in Chinese; user-entered text is not translated.
- Legal terms and descriptions preserve the current Chinese source draft; the translation does not establish legal completeness.
